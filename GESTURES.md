# GESTURES (track/systems) - conflict resolutions only

Each row: what could collide, and how it is resolved. All thresholds are reversible defaults (opts in the named module).

1. **Spiral vs any one-finger drag on bare ground** - only a finished stroke that analyzes as a spiral (stroke.js isSpiral: >= ~1.5 turns, growing radius) spawns; it is judged on lift, so drags / herding / strokes that are not spirals keep their old meaning. Hold-still-on-bare-ground spawn is OFF by default (switch in the dev panel), so the spiral is the one spawn gesture.
2. **Hook ('?') vs other strokes** - a hook alone does nothing for 1.6 s; only a following DOT under it (= '?') or '<' / '>' (= undo / redo) completes it. Anything else lets the hook lapse.
3. **3-finger tap (undo) / 4-finger tap (redo) vs 2-finger zoom / twist and ohm touches** - counted only when every finger is up within 400 ms and none moved 30 px; the 3rd finger landing takes all touches from zoom / ohms (and inside the river: from painting - strokes in progress are cancelled, not committed).
4. **3-finger 2 s still hold (dev panel) vs 3-finger tap** - the tap needs all up within 400 ms; the hold shows a filling ring and fires at 2 s. Moving 30 px, a 4th finger or lifting early cancels.
5. **Two-finger twist (river) vs pinch zoom** - twist only on bare ground with both fingers unowned and a rotation > 1.3 rad before the pinch distance changes much; otherwise zoom keeps it. Inside the river, pinch closed (< .45) or twist back closes it; spread zooms the river (1-16 bars).
6. **Mouth double tap vs strikes / herding** - the double tap must land both taps on the mouth zone within 350 ms / 40 px; the taps still sound the ohm (not consumed). Mouth control ends with another double tap or 20 s of quiet.
7. **Walking stick vs tap-to-close nodes and the current** - the stick claims a touch only while your ohm's nodes are open, on the empty left half, more than 1.25 R from the body; a short tap there still closes the nodes (passed on as a touch tap). A fast flick up (> 35 px, > 1.1 px/ms) = jump, not a stick move.
8. **Inside the river (DAW)** - drag along a lane = paint; rub = erase; drag on a bead = move it, resting 220 ms on a bead = paint over it; holding the lane's left edge 600 ms = mute; dragging out from the left edge = draw loudness / brightness (row 19). While the river is up its touches never reach the world underneath.
9. **Fuse vs drag / throw** - only a dragged ohm, pressed into another (overlap < 0.8 x the radii), nearly still, for 0.9 s fuses (ring fills); passing over or throwing past does nothing. Pulling a fused member more than 2.2 x its link length tears it off.
10. **Plants** - drawn only, never hit-tested: touching a plant = touching bare ground.
11. **Teach line (A teaches B) vs stir / loop select / spiral / hook / herding** - judged on lift, before the spiral and hook: the stroke must START in A's halo (0-30 px outside A's touch circle, the ring where a touch does not hit A; A is fixed when the finger goes down, because the stroke stirs ohms), END on another ohm B, and be a line (path length <= 1.6 x the straight distance). Loops, spirals and hooks are never that straight, and a stroke that ends on bare ground or back on A keeps its old meaning (stir / loop select). The stir while drawing is unchanged. Undo with a 3-finger tap.
12. **Breeding** - no gesture: two ohms that keep jamming (autojam pair, presence >= .45 for 8 bars) breed. Bringing two ohms together (drag / throw) is how you introduce them, so nothing else changes. Out of key = they refuse (a flicker and a push apart), not a child.
13. **Flick vs strike / grab / throw** - a touch on an ohm still strikes it. A flick is a one-finger touch that starts on an ohm, is the last finger up, and lasts under 220 ms while moving over 24 px faster than 0.5 px/ms. It sends the ohm off at the finger's speed (the last ~60 ms). A slower drag is a grab, and letting go of a grab is the old throw (the ohm keeps its spring speed). Taps (< 12 px, mouth double tap) and the walking stick's jump flick (owned touches on bare ground) never count as flicks.
14. **Squish vs fuse** - pressing a dragged ohm into the ground or a world edge squishes it (flatter, and it sounds like a squeeze). Pressing it into another ohm is still the fuse (row 9). Squish needs no hold and leaves nothing behind: let go and it wobbles back.
15. **Stretch / pinch shape** - display and sound only (no new touch): a dragged ohm stretches toward a fast finger, a flying ohm stretches along its flight, and the existing two-finger pinch now also squeezes or swells the body. Pinch held still = sing is unchanged.
16. **Drag a drawn instrument's end / rim vs stroke, strum, grab** (315d647) - one finger that lands within 16 px of a drawn string or bar end, or on a drawn drum's rim band, and NOT on an ohm (ohms win), with the walk nodes closed and the River shut: dragging retunes it (length / size sets pitch). It claims only after 6 px; a still touch there is the old tap (it plays). Panel switch.
17. **Quick wide spread on a fused group vs two grabs, pinch, zoom, twist** (6756e6e) - two fingers that start on two DIFFERENT ohms of one stuck-together group and spread >= 120 px within 350 ms split the group. It only watches: each finger still drags its own ohm, so slow or small spreads are the old two drags (and the old tear). Pinch on ONE ohm (squeeze, DNA past 1.8) and the bare-ground zoom / twist never see it. Panel switch.
18. **Carry a drawn thing (Q354) vs fuse, squish, bumps, end drag, tap** - pick up: drag an ohm onto a drawn thing and hold it there nearly still for 0.9 s (the fuse's press; if the ohm is also pressed into another ohm, the fuse wins and nothing is picked up). Moving across a thing is the old bump (it sounds). Take: one finger on the CARRIED thing (not on an ohm: ohms win; not on a string end: row 16 wins) moved >= 10 px takes it off and puts it where you let go; a still touch there is the old tap (it plays). Knock off: another ohm hitting it fast. Drop: a flick or a hard throw of the carrier (row 13 unchanged; the thing just stays behind). Attach / take undo with a 3-finger tap. Panel switch.
19. **River automation (Q357 d) vs mute hold, paint, bead move, zoom / close, undo** - inside the river, a finger that lands on a lane's LEFT EDGE (x < 44 px, the colour dot) and DRAGS OUT more than 14 px draws an automation line for that lane: from the edge's upper half = brightness (filter, cyan), from its lower half = loudness (white); height in the lane = level, left-right = time, looping in the lane's own cycle (its painted loop's length, else the smallest of 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 bars that holds it). Before this, that drag did nothing (it only cancelled the mute hold), so no gesture is taken away: holding the edge still 600 ms is still mute, touches that START inside the lane still paint / erase / move beads, a second finger still makes it a two-finger zoom / close, 3 / 4 fingers still undo / redo. A flat line along the top clears that line. Switch: dawAuto (ON).
20. **River bounce (Q358 a) vs everything inside the river** - inside the river, one finger HELD STILL 0.9 s on the empty strip BELOW the lanes renders the whole song in the river to a WAV and opens the share sheet (web: a download). A ring fills while holding; moving > 12 px, lifting early, or a second finger landing (zoom / close) cancels it; 3 / 4 fingers still undo / redo. That strip had no touch before, so nothing is taken away; lanes, their left edges (mute / automation, row 19) and beads are untouched. Switch: bounce (ON).
21. **Conducting (V07 B / R4.11, conduct.js) vs ground shaping with your voice (v0.0.13 T1 A), undo / dev hold** - NOT RESOLVED, listed for Wilbow. The old gesture "two fingers resting still on bare ground ~0.6 s raise the baton" is the SAME touch as "shape the ground with your voice". The conducting engine is on (switch conduct); its touch binding is behind 'conductGesture' (OFF). With it ON the still hold raises the baton (no ground shaping) and a THIRD finger's strokes conduct: a stroke moves well past 30 px, so it is never the 3-finger tap (undo) and it cancels the 3-finger dev hold; zoom already lets go when a 3rd finger lands. Lifting a baton finger releases. Options to decide: a which one gets the still hold; b conduct with the hold on an OHM-free spot only after a jam is playing nearby (context); c a different raise (e.g. a hold + short upstroke).
22. **Egg stroke grammar (V07 A, not built)** - strokes that start on a just-hatched ohm (taps = drummer, wave = pad, circle = ringer, flick = pluck) collide with tap-to-strike, grab / drag and flick-to-fly on that ohm. Needs a decision before it is built (e.g. only inside the 1.5 s window and only on the newborn).
